Questions

Is my date of birth saved?

No. The age is worked out on your device. The dates you type are never sent or saved, and they are cleared when you close the page.

Can I find my age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the "Age on" date to see the exact age on that day, for example the cut-off date in an exam notice.

How are people born on 29 February handled?

In years without 29 February, the birthday is counted on 28 February.
